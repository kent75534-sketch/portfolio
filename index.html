Professional Narrative

From a government school in Delhi to IIM Shillong built on economics, tested in real markets, and driven by an uncompromising belief that rigorous analysis is the only honest foundation for advice.

The Beginning

I did not come from finance. I came from economics the kind you study because you genuinely want to understand how the world works, not just how markets move. Growing up in Delhi, attending a government school, then earning my place at Hansraj College as Economics Subject Topper none of it was handed to me. That foundation built something no MBA course can: the habit of intellectual rigour.

My first real exposure to markets was not an internship. It was a CAPM research project where I found that 90% of Indian IT sector stocks were systematically beating their expected returns and that Mphasis and Mindtree were undervalued by 701% and 268% against benchmarks. That project told me two things: markets misprice constantly, and data almost always reveals what consensus misses. I have been chasing that edge ever since.
